## Entry

> The lift mirror shows me leaving before I have pressed the button, and for a moment I am not sure which of us is going home.

## The idea

A lift mirror is the most ordinary surface in the most ordinary wait — yet it doubles us every day without us noticing. The sentence twists that doubling one step further: the reflection acts first, leaving before the button is pressed, so the familiar commute home briefly splits into two people and one uncertain destination. The strangeness stays grounded in a real, everyday moment everyone has stood through.
